# PF301: User script in a job that holds credentials

A job that holds secrets, an OIDC token or write permissions, or that runs under a privileged trigger (`issue_comment`, `issues`, `pull_request_target`, …), can't contain your own `run:` steps. Steps in a job share its token, secrets and files, so any script there, and anything it installs, could steal them. This is how most real CI compromises happen: a script in a privileged job runs attacker-influenced code.

## How to fix it

Split the job: do the scripted work in an unprivileged job that `produces` an artifact, and `consumes` it in the privileged job, which only runs effects and reviewed actions. Effects (`comment`, `add_labels`, `release`, …) cover the common privileged operations without a script.
