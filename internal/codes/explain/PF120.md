# PF120: Unknown or unsupported trigger

The trigger under `on:` isn't known, or isn't supported in this version.

Pinfold v0.1 compiles `push`, `schedule`, `manual` (workflow_dispatch) and the privileged triggers `issue_comment`, `issues`, `pull_request_target` (metadata-only jobs), `pull_request_review`, `pull_request_review_comment` and `discussion`/`discussion_comment`. Untrusted `pull_request` builds arrive in v0.2.

## How to fix it

Use a supported trigger. Keep workflows Pinfold can't express hand-written; Pinfold leaves them alone.
