# PF304: Action not allowed in a job that holds credentials

Jobs that hold credentials may only use actions from Pinfold's registry that are marked `privileged_ok`: reviewed for that major version, running no repository-controlled code, and with no unpinned nested references. The action is unknown, its major version wasn't reviewed, or it doesn't meet the rule (the hint says which).

## How to fix it

Move the step to an unprivileged job, use an effect, or open an issue asking for the action (and version) to be reviewed for the registry.
