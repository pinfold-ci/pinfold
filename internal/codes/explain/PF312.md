# PF312: workflow_run workflows aren't supported

User-written `workflow_run` workflows run with full credentials on the results of other workflows, often including artifacts from forks, and are a common way to build a pwn request. Pinfold v0.1 doesn't compile them. (The generated `pinfold-verify.yml` is the one exception, because the compiler writes it.)

## How to fix it

Keep the workflow hand-written for now, or restructure it around effects.
