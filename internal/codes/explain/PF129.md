# PF129: Checkout after artifact downloads

A job that `consumes:` artifacts has a `checkout` step that isn't its first step.

Pinfold downloads consumed artifacts at the start of the job, right after a leading checkout. Checking out cleans the workspace, so a checkout later in the job would delete the downloaded files, or mix them with the repository's files in ways that are hard to review.

## How to fix it

Make the checkout the job's first step:

```yaml
steps:
  - checkout: {}
  - run: make package
```
