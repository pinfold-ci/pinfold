# PF127: Artifact consumed from a job that isn't a dependency

`consumes: { name: { from: job } }` names a job that this job doesn't depend on (directly or through other jobs' `needs`). The artifact might not exist yet when this job runs, and the digest check that protects it couldn't be wired up.

## How to fix it

Add the producing job to this job's `needs:` (directly or through another job).
